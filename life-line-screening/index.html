Quick answers

Does this replace seeing my doctor?

No — and it's not meant to. These are screenings, not diagnoses. They flag potential risk so you and your doctor can follow up. Think of it as an early-warning system, not a doctor replacement.

What screenings are included?

Packages typically cover carotid artery screening (stroke risk), heart rhythm check (atrial fibrillation), abdominal aortic aneurysm screening, peripheral artery disease check, and an osteoporosis risk assessment. Exact packages and pricing are on the official site.

Where do screenings take place?

At local community venues — churches, community centers, hotels — in thousands of locations across the US. You enter your ZIP on their site to see upcoming events near you.
