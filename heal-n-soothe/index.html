Quick answers

Is this a painkiller?

No. It's a dietary supplement, not a drug — it doesn't work like ibuprofen or acetaminophen. It's designed to support joint comfort over time as part of a daily routine, not to numb pain on the spot.

How long before I notice anything?

Supplements like this are a weeks-not-hours thing. Most people who stick with enzyme formulas evaluate them over 30–60 days of consistent daily use. Anyone promising overnight miracles is overselling it.

Are there side effects to know about?

Systemic enzymes are generally well tolerated, but they can interact with blood-thinning medication and aren't for everyone. If you're on prescriptions or managing a condition, check with your doctor first — that's just common sense with any supplement.
