Quick answers

Will creatine make me bulky or bloated?

Creatine supports strength and power — it doesn't build bulk on its own; that comes from training and eating. Some people hold a little extra water in muscles when they start, which is normal and harmless. It works the same for men and women.

When should I take it?

Timing matters less than consistency. Daily use is what saturates your muscles over 3–4 weeks — most people just mix it into their post-workout shake or morning routine and don't overthink the clock.

Is it just for bodybuilders?

No. Creatine is one of the most researched supplements across sports — sprinters, lifters, and active adults use it. And the collagen side supports joints and skin, which matters whether or not you compete in anything.
