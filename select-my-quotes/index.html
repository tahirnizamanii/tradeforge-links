Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes, for homeowners. The business model is lead generation: contractors pay to receive your project details and compete for the job. You never pay the service anything.

Will I get spammed with calls?

Matched contractors may contact you — that's the point of requesting quotes. If you'd rather not be contacted, don't submit the form. Only request quotes when you're genuinely considering the project.

Do I have to hire someone?

No. There's zero obligation. Many people use the quotes purely to learn the market rate before deciding anything — that's a perfectly good use of it.
